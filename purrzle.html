Privacy Policy

Purrzle · LOGIRA · Effective 11 October 2026

This policy explains how the Purrzle app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by LOGIRA ("we", "us"), based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and, for players in the European Economic Area, the EU GDPR.

In short: there are no accounts and we never ask for your name or email. Your progress stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and, if you allow it, sends anonymous usage stats and crash reports to PostHog so we can fix bugs. You can change both choices in the app's Settings.

1. Information stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, daily results, stats, settings, the cats you collect and any cat you design in the Cat Studio are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us. Resetting progress in Settings or uninstalling the app removes them.

2. Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone. No information is sent anywhere to deliver it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a hint, an extra heart, or to open the Cat Studio early. When ads are shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • how you interact with the app and with ads (such as app launches, ads viewed or tapped);
  • diagnostic information (such as app launch time and performance).

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads.

UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the app shows Google's consent form. Personalised ads, and storing or reading information such as your advertising ID on your device for advertising, happen only if you consent. If you do not consent, Google shows "limited ads", which do not use your advertising ID for personalisation and use data stored on your device only to prevent fraud and abuse.

United States: in states with consumer privacy laws, the app shows Google's message for those laws, where you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising.

You can change your ad choices at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings. Ads in the app are limited to content rated for general audiences. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Anonymous usage stats and crash reports (PostHog)

If you allow it, the app sends anonymous usage stats and crash reports to PostHog, which processes them on our behalf on servers in the European Union. This includes:

  • a random ID created when the app is installed and stored on your device (not linked to your name, email, advertising ID or any account);
  • game events, such as puzzles started and completed, hints used, and when an ad was shown;
  • the app version, your phone's make and model, operating system version, language and time zone;
  • crash reports and error details, so we can fix bugs.

We do not send names, free text or precise location, and the app tells PostHog not to work out your location from your IP address. This data is used only to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. It is never used for advertising.

Your choice: in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the app asks before sending anything. Elsewhere it is on by default. Everywhere, you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data, and nothing more is sent.

5. Legal basis

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising, for storing or reading information on your device for advertising, and, in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, for anonymous usage stats and crash reports. We rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game for showing limited ads, for preventing advertising fraud, and for usage stats where consent is not required. Storing your progress on your phone is necessary to provide the game.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with Google (for ads, as described above) and PostHog (as our processor for usage stats and crash reports). Under some US state laws, using data for personalised ads can count as "sharing"; you can opt out as described in section 3. Google may process data outside the UK and the EU; Google uses appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, for these transfers.

7. Data retention

Information on your phone stays until you reset progress or uninstall the app. Usage stats and crash reports are kept only as long as our PostHog project's retention settings allow, then deleted. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Children

Purrzle is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content rated for general audiences.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the app, you can turn off usage data in Settings → Privacy, reset or delete the advertising ID in the phone's settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes everything it stored on the phone. Because there are no accounts and usage data is anonymous, we cannot find or delete a specific player's data.

9. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of some US states have similar rights, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we hold no account or contact details for you, most choices are made directly in the app (Settings → Privacy) or with Google. You can also contact us using the details below. In the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk; in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
