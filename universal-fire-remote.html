Privacy Policy

Universal Fire Remote · LOGIRA · Effective 10 October 2026

This policy explains how the Universal Fire Remote app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by LOGIRA ("we", "us"), based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

In short: we do not run any servers for the app, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect or store your personal information ourselves. The app talks directly to your TV over your home network. If ads are shown, Google AdMob collects some device data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

To reconnect to your TVs, the app saves the name, local network (IP) address and pairing token of each Fire TV device you pair, plus which device you used last. This information is stored only on your phone, in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). It is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by removing the device in the app or uninstalling the app.

2. Local network access

The app uses your local Wi-Fi network to find Fire TV devices and send remote-control commands to them. These commands, including any text you type with the app's keyboard, go directly from your phone to your TV on your own network. We do not receive, see or store them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and may show ads provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version);
  • ad interactions (such as ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. We use Google's consent management platform to ask for and record your choices.

  • UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland: when you first open the app, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to your data being used for personalised ads and the other purposes it lists. You can choose Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options to make choices for each purpose and partner, including objecting to processing based on legitimate interest. No ads are requested until you have made a choice. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads, which rely on limited data such as your approximate location and the content of the app.
  • United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Utah), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted (interest-based) advertising. Open Privacy settings in the app and choose Don't sell or share my data. After you opt out you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised using your data from this app.

Where these choices apply to you, a Privacy settings button appears on the app's main screen so you can review or change your choice at any time.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Legal basis

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest in funding a free app for non-personalised advertising and fraud prevention. Storing your paired devices on your phone is necessary to provide the app's core function.

5. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share any information with anyone other than Google and the advertising partners shown in Google's consent form, as described above. Under some US state laws, allowing advertising partners to use data for targeted advertising can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; you can opt out of this as described in section 3. Google may process data outside the UK; Google uses appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, for these transfers.

6. Data retention

Paired-device information stays on your phone until you remove it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are best handled in the app (removing devices or changing Privacy settings) or directly with Google. You can also contact us using the details below. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

United States residents: depending on the state you live in, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to access, correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. You can opt out at any time from Privacy settings in the app (see section 3). For other requests, contact us using the details below; if we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our response. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.
